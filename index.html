The Five Steps

From first call to closing day.

A dated path from first call to wire, so you always know what happens next. Here is what working with me actually looks like.

  1. 01

    First call

    15 minutes

    Fill out the form or call me at (949) 438-5948. Fifteen minutes on your home, your timeline, and your number. You hang up knowing whether listing now makes sense.

  2. 02

    The walk-through

    At your home

    I walk every room and price what I see. You leave with a written list: the repairs that pay for themselves at closing, the ones to skip, and the number your home clears if we touch nothing. Prep list and price in hand before you sign anything.

  3. 03

    Your market analysis

    Within 24 hours

    Within 24 hours you have my written analysis: the comps, the competition, the price, and your exact net. You decide if and when to list.

  4. 04

    Launch

    Within 7 days

    Once you sign, your home is live within 7 days, often in 48 to 72 hours. Professional photography, drone, walk-through video, MLS syndication, social campaigns, and a dedicated property page. All on a fixed timeline you can hold me to.

  5. 05

    Negotiate and close

    30 to 45 days

    Every offer scored in writing: your net and its certainty to close. Then I carry every inspection, contingency, and milestone to the finish. 30 to 45 days later, the wire hits your account.

  6. Total timeline

    Six to ten weeks from listing to keys.

    In Orange County a well-priced home goes under contract in 14 to 30 days, then 30 to 45 days of escrow. You get your own dated timeline on the first call.
